# Privacy Policy for Enchanted Labyrinth

**Last updated: October 19, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how our game handles user information.

## Information We Process

Our game does not collect, store, or process any traditional personal information from users, such as your real name, email address, phone number, or physical address. You are not required to provide any personal credentials to play the game.

However, to provide core game functionality, save your game progress (such as current levels), and manage anonymous sessions, the game utilizes unique technical identifiers.

Through Google Firebase services, the following technical data is processed:

* **User IDs:** Anonymous account tokens generated by Firebase Auth to manage your guest session.
* **Device or other IDs:** Technical installation identifiers used by Firebase to recognize your device.
* **App interactions:** Information regarding your in-game interactions, specifically saving your current game level.

This data is used strictly for internal app functionality—specifically to ensure you do not lose your game progress when you close or restart the application.

## Data Sharing

We do not collect or share personal information with third parties. We do not sell, rent, or share any user data or identifiers with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing companies. All processed technical data is used solely within our Firebase infrastructure to support game operations.

## Children's Privacy

Our game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The technical identifiers processed by Firebase are used purely to support the internal operations of the game (saving progress).

## Third-Party Services

The game uses Google Firebase to manage anonymous user sessions and store current game levels. This third-party service processes data in compliance with its own privacy standards. All data transfer is automatically encrypted in transit over a secure connection.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if necessary. Any changes will be posted on this page.
